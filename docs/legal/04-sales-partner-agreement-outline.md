# Sales Partner (Independent Contractor) Agreement — OUTLINE v0.1

> **DRAFT OUTLINE FOR LAWYER AND ACCOUNTANT REVIEW. NOT LEGAL OR TAX ADVICE.** Salespeople are treated as **contractors to start with** (decision D-16). Whether a person is legally a contractor or an employee depends on the actual working relationship, not on the label, and misclassification carries legal and tax consequences. **[LAWYER/ACCOUNTANT: classification test in each country, superannuation/tax obligations, and whether commission-only arrangements are permitted for the role]**.

## 1. Parties and status
Cheery [entity] and [Partner name/entity, ABN, GST status]. Independent contractor; no authority to bind Cheery; no employment, partnership or agency; free to work for others subject to conflict and confidentiality terms **[LAWYER: the degree of control Cheery exerts (hours, exclusivity, tools, supervision) affects classification — keep consistent with the label]**.

## 2. Services
Identify and introduce prospective restaurant customers; register leads in the Cheery partner portal; present Cheery using approved materials only; no promises beyond the published plans, no unauthorized discounts or custom terms; follow the code of conduct.

## 3. Lead registration and ownership
- Leads are registered in the portal with prospect details and date.
- **Cheery management decides lead ownership** (decision D-15); conflicting claims are resolved by management with a recorded reason; decision is final within the agreement **[LAWYER: fairness and dispute process]**.
- A lead lapses if not progressed within [ ] days unless management extends.

## 4. Commission
- **Plan per partner**, set in the Commission Plan Schedule (versioned, effective-dated): rate (default 30%), **revenue basis** (subscription / add-ons / setup fees; before or after tax and discounts), **duration** (default 12 months from the customer's first paid invoice), clawback window (default 90 days), minimum payout balance, payout cadence (default monthly).
- Commission accrues only on amounts **actually received** from the customer for the Cheery subscription and only for customers whose ownership has been confirmed.
- **Clawback:** commission is reversed if the customer cancels, is refunded, charges back, or fails to pay within the clawback window; reversals are netted against future payouts, and where there are no future payouts the partner repays within [ ] days **[LAWYER: enforceability, deductions from payments]**.
- Plan changes apply prospectively and never rewrite commission already earned.
- Statements are available in the portal; disputes must be raised within [ ] days.
- Cheery may change standard terms with [60] days' notice; changes to an individual plan require agreement.

## 5. Payment and tax
- Payment by bank transfer after approval (maker–checker) on the monthly run, subject to minimum balance.
- Partner is responsible for their own tax, GST, insurance, superannuation and registrations; provides a valid ABN and tax invoice where required (otherwise withholding may apply) **[ACCOUNTANT]**.
- Payments in the partner's country currency per the plan; foreign exchange and fees as stated **[ACCOUNTANT]**.

## 6. Confidentiality and customer data
- Partners **do not receive access to customer guest data or operational data**; they see only commercial status of their own customers (no booking or guest information).
- Confidential information (pricing, product roadmap, customer lists) must not be disclosed or used outside the agreement.

## 7. Conduct and compliance
Honest dealing; no misleading statements about certification, security, availability or compliance (e.g. never claim ISO 27001/SOC 2 certification or 24×7 support unless true); anti-bribery and conflicts of interest; compliance with marketing and spam laws (no unsolicited bulk messages); comply with Cheery brand guidelines.

## 8. Intellectual property
Cheery's materials and marks remain Cheery's; limited licence to use approved materials; any partner-created sales materials using Cheery marks require approval.

## 9. Term and termination
Term [12 months], renewable; termination for convenience on [30] days' notice; immediate termination for serious breach; on termination, commission continues for the remaining duration on customers whose ownership was confirmed (subject to clawback and ongoing compliance) **[LAWYER/commercial]**; return of materials and portal access removed.

## 10. Liability and insurance
Mutual limitations; partner responsible for own insurance [professional indemnity/public liability as required] **[LAWYER]**.
